☕ Global Coffee Traceability Platform

A modern AI-powered digital ecosystem designed for the global coffee industry. This platform enhances coffee traceability, farm management, sustainability, and supply chain transparency through smart technologies, real-time analytics, GPS mapping, and AI-driven insights.

🌍 Vision

The Global Coffee Traceability Platform aims to revolutionize the coffee supply chain by connecting farmers, cooperatives, exporters, buyers, and coffee professionals through a secure, transparent, and intelligent digital infrastructure.

The platform is built for international scalability and accessibility, supporting users from different countries, languages, and production systems worldwide.
